# Tactics troubleshooting (compatibility page)

The maintained guide has moved to
[Troubleshooting direct automation](../direct/troubleshooting.md).

It starts from the public `leancert` / `leancert?` workflow and covers current
semantic-router diagnostics. Lower-level dedicated-tactic and older-release
messages, including historical `native_decide` and reflected-expression
errors, are retained in its advanced section.
